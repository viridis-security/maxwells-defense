# Maxwell's Defense code of conduct

We welcome rigorous technical and scientific disagreement. Be respectful, state
uncertainty, credit sources, protect privacy and data sovereignty, and criticize
claims rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, retaliation, and pressure to weaken evidence, rights, or
safety gates are prohibited.

Report ordinary concerns to the maintainers. Use the private route in
`SECURITY.md` for sensitive concerns and mark the report **Code of Conduct —
confidential**. Do not publish private information in an issue.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect people and
the evidence record. Conflicted maintainers must recuse themselves. A reason
will be provided when safety and law permit.
