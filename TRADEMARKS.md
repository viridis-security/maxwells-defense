## What needs written permission

- A product, service, fork, package or domain name that contains a Viridis mark or a confusingly similar
  name.
- Offering a Viridis project as a hosted service under a Viridis mark.
- Co-branding, use of a Viridis logo, or any statement that Viridis endorses, reviewed or operates your
  work.

## Certificates and receipts

Anyone can run the open verifier and check any certificate or receipt against the open standard, free.
Certificates and receipts from a self-hosted deployment are self-rooted and must be labeled that way.
Only Viridis's production trust root issues Viridis-signed receipts and certificates, and only those may
carry **Certified by ViridisOS** or another Viridis seal.

Request mark-use, co-branding or cross-certification terms through https://viridisconservation.com.
Nothing here limits uses the law allows, such as nominative fair use.
